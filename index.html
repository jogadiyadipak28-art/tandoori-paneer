Our Story

When the tandoor calls you home

At Ember & Spice, we believe comfort food isn't about complexity — it's about memory. Tandoori Paneer Tikka is the dish that fills kitchens across India with the scent of cumin, coriander, and smoldering charcoal.

We marinate thick cubes of fresh paneer in yogurt, Kashmiri chili, and a whisper of garam masala, then thread them with crisp bell peppers and purple onion before they meet the flame. Every char mark tells a story of warmth, gathering, and the simple joy of sharing skewers straight from the grill.

"Some dishes feed the body. Tandoori paneer feeds the soul."

The Recipe

Make it at home

This is the recipe we've been perfecting for years — simple enough for a weeknight, special enough for guests. No tandoor? A hot grill or oven broiler works beautifully.

Serves 4 Vegetarian Gluten-free

Marinade

  • 400g paneer, cubed
  • ½ cup thick yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp garam masala & chaat masala
  • Juice of 1 lemon, salt to taste

Skewers & Grill

  1. Marinate paneer for at least 2 hours (overnight is best).
  2. Thread paneer with onion and bell pepper on skewers.
  3. Grill on high heat, turning until charred on all sides.
  4. Garnish with cilantro, serve with mint chutney and lemon wedges.
